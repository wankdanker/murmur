Set up Murmur

Murmur turns speech into text in any text box. Recognition runs on this computer, so your audio never leaves it.

  1. Allow microphone access below. Chrome asks once for the extension, not for every site.
  2. Click into a text box and press the mic button that appears, or press Alt+Shift+D.
  3. Speak. Text is typed when you pause. Click the button again or press Esc to stop.

The first dictation downloads the speech model (about 60–150 MB) from Hugging Face. After that it works offline.
